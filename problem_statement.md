# 💼 InternLite – Internship Discovery & Application Platform

## 1. Title

**InternLite – Internship Discovery & Application Platform**

## 2. Domain

**Education Technology / Internship Marketplace / Recruitment**

## 3. Users

- **Student:** Creates a profile, manages skills and resume, searches internships, applies for opportunities, and tracks applications.
- **Recruiter:** Creates a company profile, posts internships, reviews applications, shortlists candidates, and manages interviews.
- **Admin:** Manages students, recruiters, companies, internships, applications, and platform activities.

## 4. Problem Statement

Students often search for internships across multiple websites, social media platforms, college groups, and company career pages. This makes it difficult to discover relevant opportunities, manage applications, and track application status in one place. Recruiters also need an efficient platform to publish internship opportunities and manage applications from multiple candidates. InternLite provides a centralized platform connecting students with internship opportunities and helping recruiters manage the internship hiring process.

## 5. Proposed Solution

The application will provide:

- Student registration and login
- Student profile management
- Skills and education management
- Resume management
- Internship posting
- Internship search and filtering
- Internship details
- Internship application
- Saved/bookmarked internships
- Application status tracking
- Recruiter/company management
- Candidate application management
- Candidate shortlisting
- Interview scheduling
- Notifications
- Admin dashboard
- AI-powered internship recommendations

## 7. User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| **Student** | Manage profile, skills and resume, search internships, save internships, apply, and track applications |
| **Recruiter** | Manage company profile, post internships, view applications, shortlist candidates, and schedule interviews |
| **Admin** | Manage users, companies, internships, applications, reports, and platform activities |

## 8. Success Criteria

- User can register and log in.
- Student can create and complete a professional profile.
- Recruiter can create a company profile.
- Recruiter can post an internship.
- Student can search and filter internships.
- Student can apply for an internship.
- Student can save internships for later.
- Student can track application status.
- Recruiter can view and manage applications.
- Recruiter can shortlist candidates.
- Recruiter can schedule interviews.
- Notifications are sent for important application updates.
- Admin can monitor and manage the platform.
- System can recommend relevant internships to students.

## 9. Out of Scope

- Guaranteed internship placement
- Guaranteed employment
- Salary/stipend payment processing
- Payroll management
- Background verification
- Government employment portal integration
- Complete HR management system
- Employee attendance management
- Employee performance management
- Native Android/iOS applications
- Real-time video interview system
- Automated hiring decisions
- Legal contract generation
